Worth leaving Alberta for?

The Canadian Rockies are the benchmark precisely because they are world class. Every destination is placed by how much of it you cannot get within a day of Edmonton. Mountains alone earn nothing here; deserts, canyons, coasts, geysers, volcanoes and dunes earn a lot.
